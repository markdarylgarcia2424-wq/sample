Information and Communication Technology

History

Information and Communication Technology (ICT) covers all the tools we use to create, store, share, and communicate information. People first shared information through speech and drawings, then through writing, printing, the telegraph, telephone, radio, television, computers, and finally the Internet.

Technology That Existed Before Modern Technology

  • Cave paintings and symbols
  • Smoke signals and drums
  • Carrier pigeons and messengers
  • Papyrus, paper, and the printing press
  • Abacus (early calculating tool)
  • Telegraph and telephone

Inventor

  • Johannes Gutenberg – printing press
  • Samuel Morse – telegraph and Morse code
  • Alexander Graham Bell – telephone
  • Charles Babbage – early mechanical computer design
  • Tim Berners-Lee – World Wide Web

Conclusion

ICT has changed how people learn, work, and connect with one another. From simple smoke signals to the Internet, each invention built on the one before it, and understanding this history helps us use today's technology wisely.
